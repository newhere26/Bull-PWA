Build a polished mobile-first PWA called Reclaim.

Concept

Reclaim helps people replace mindless scrolling with meaningful activities.

The core loop is:

I have X minutes → choose my mood → get 3 things I could do → start one → complete it → see my reclaimed time accumulate.

The app should feel calm, premium, modern and encouraging — not like a productivity app or screen-time blocker.

V1 screens

1. Onboarding

* Explain the core idea: even 30 min/day = ~15 hours/month.
* Ask approximate daily scrolling time.
* Ask interests: Reading, Learning, Fitness, Creativity, Music, Outdoors, Mindfulness, Cooking, Puzzles, Social, Building, Curiosity.
* Ask when they usually scroll.
* Keep onboarding short.

2. Home
Show prominently:

* Time reclaimed today
* Time reclaimed this month
* Time reclaimed this year
* Large CTA: “I have some time”

Also include “Add reclaimed time” for manually logging time spent doing something instead of scrolling.

3. Time selection
5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 60+ minutes + custom.

4. Mood
Low energy / Relax / Curious / Energetic / Creative / Social / Surprise me.

5. Recommendations
Show exactly 3 activity suggestions based on:

* available time
* mood
* interests
* previous activity history

Each activity needs a title, short description and estimated duration.

Include “Give me different options.”

6. Activity
Simple timer + activity instructions + Finish button.

When completed:

You reclaimed 18 minutes.

Update the user’s total automatically.

7. History
Show completed activities and reclaimed time by day/category.

8. Weekly summary
Show total reclaimed time, categories and most-used activities. Give simple rule-based insights when enough data exists.

Activity database

Seed at least 150 genuinely varied activities, ranging from 5 minutes to 60+ minutes.

Include productive AND purely enjoyable activities. Don’t make everything about self-improvement.

Each activity should have:

* duration
* category
* mood
* energy level
* interests
* indoor/outdoor
* equipment requirements

Create a simple recommendation algorithm rather than AI for V1.

Design

Mobile-first, approximately 375–430px wide.

Use:

* clean typography
* generous whitespace
* attractive cards
* subtle animations
* large touch targets
* light/dark mode

Avoid:

* excessive gamification
* streaks
* leaderboards
* social feeds
* infinite scrolling
* guilt/shame messaging
* generic productivity-dashboard aesthetics

The app itself should not become another thing to scroll through.

Important

Do NOT build app blocking, social features, subscriptions, AI recommendations, wearable integration or browser extensions in V1.

Prioritize a complete, polished core experience over lots of features.

The most important metric is:

Minutes of intentional activity Reclaim helps users create.

Build the app so the entire experience works end-to-end with real data persistence, not mock buttons or static screens.
